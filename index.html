Connect Your Cloud Storage

One-time setup — your data will sync across all your devices

Step-by-Step Setup (free, ~5 min)
1
Go to firebase.google.com and click Get Started — sign in with your Google account
2
Click Create a project, give it any name (e.g. "my-study-tracker"), turn off Google Analytics, click Create
3
Inside your project click the </> web icon, give it a nickname, click Register app — copy the firebaseConfig object shown
4
In the left sidebar click Firestore Database → Create database → Start in test mode, pick any location, click Done
5
Paste your firebaseConfig below and click Connect

Your config is saved locally on this device — you won't be asked again
